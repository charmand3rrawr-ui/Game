# How every sprite is drawn

GENERATED by `pnpm run assets` — do not edit by hand.

These rules apply to **every** entry in `manifest.jsonl`. Each entry carries only what is
specific to it; nothing below is repeated there.

## Projection and light

- Isometric 2:1, the same fixed camera angle for every sprite in the game.
- Lit from the upper right, one consistent light direction, so a settlement reads as one scene.
- Transparent background. No baseplate, no ground, no baked shadow — the renderer draws the plot
  and the contact shadow underneath.
- Nothing outside the subject's own footprint.

## What must NOT be drawn in

Seven overlay axes are composited at runtime (`Visual_Overlays`), and drawing any of them into the
sprite would double them up or contradict live state:

- **Era retrofit** (7 (Era I–VII)) — Material and technology pass over the same silhouette: thatch → tile → slate → corrugated steel → composite → alloy → lattice. A Farm in a galactic empire is still a Farm, but it has drone seeders and a fusion tap.
- **Culture / Ideology** (9 (3 axes × 3 positions)) — Authority reads as symmetry, columns, banners. Liberty reads as open frontage, mixed use, civic art. Faith reads as shrines and iconography. Reason reads as glass, instrumentation, clean geometry.
- **Cultivation aura** (7 (realm bands)) — Ambient VFX intensity around cultivation and high-grade buildings: none → faint motes → flowing currents → standing arcs → local weather → gravity distortion → visible time-dilation shimmer.
- **Damage state** (4) — Pristine / scarred (scorch, patched walls) / breached (structural loss, exposed interior) / ruined (collapsed, smoking, salvageable). Damage states persist visibly until repaired.
- **Activity state** (3) — Idle (dark, still, no staff) / working (animated, lit, staff and carts) / overdriven (shard-accelerated: temporal shimmer, blurred workers, audible hum). Overdriven is deliberately conspicuous.
- **Biome & season** (6) — Desert, temperate, tundra, tropical, volcanic, void. Affects roofing, foundations, vegetation, snow accumulation, and light temperature.
- **Heraldry** (Player-defined) — Owner banners, unit liveries, and dynasty colours applied to defined decal slots. Changes hands visibly on conquest.

So: no damage, no scaffolding or construction, no banners, liveries or heraldry, no weather or
season, no cultivation aura, and no crew or activity.

## Why tiers rather than levels

483 buildings across 1,338 levels is 646,000 states, which nobody can author. Art is drawn per
**tier** — twelve of them — and a level maps onto one. A building keeps its shape inside a tier and
visibly rebuilds when it crosses into the next, which is what makes an upgrade something a player
can see rather than only read.

| Tier | Levels | Silhouette | The player should read it as |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0–0 | Flat ground + markers | "Something is coming here" |
| 1 | 1–24 | Small, low, irregular | "Someone just started" |
| 2 | 25–96 | Doubles in height; regular geometry | "This is real now" |
| 3 | 97–224 | Spreads horizontally; annexes appear | "They know what they are doing" |
| 4 | 225–384 | Vertical emphasis begins | "This is a serious institution" |
| 5 | 385–544 | Complex massing; a tower or spire | "Wealth lives here" |
| 6 | 545–704 | Mixed-period silhouette | "Old bones, new power" |
| 7 | 705–864 | Landmark scale | "You can see it from the next province" |
| 8 | 865–1024 | Sprawling complex; auxiliaries | "An economy in one building" |
| 9 | 1025–1152 | Impossible cantilevers; floating elements | "This should not stand up" |
| 10 | 1153–1280 | Partly non-solid; auroral | "The world bends here" |
| 11 | 1281–1337 | Bespoke per building | "There are only a handful of these in existence" |

## Era

Era changes the MATERIAL, never the silhouette: "thatch to tile to steel to composite" over the
same shape. A Farm is recognisably the same farm in Era I and Era VII.
